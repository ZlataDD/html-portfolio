The Best Movies According to Zlata

My top 3 movies of all-time.

Grayhound

Greyhound follows a U.S. Navy commander on his first wartime mission escorting an Allied convoy through the perilous mid-Atlantic while hunted by German U-boats. The film is a tense, fast-paced naval thriller with authentic battle tactics and Tom Hanks delivering a grounded, commanding performance. Critics praised its lean, immersive action and historical accuracy, though some found it light on character depth, making it worthwhile for war film enthusiasts.

The Martian

The Martian follows astronaut Mark Watney, stranded on Mars after a storm leaves him presumed dead. Using science and ingenuity, he works to survive and signal Earth as NASA and international teams race to rescue him. This film stands out for blending suspense with humor, delivering an uplifting message about perseverance. Critics praised its realism, engaging pace, and Matt Damon’s charismatic performance, making it a rewarding watch.

Hidden Figures

Hidden Figures follows three African-American women at NASA who use their exceptional mathematical skills to help drive the U.S. space program forward during the Cold War, overcoming entrenched racial and gender barriers. The film offers inspiring performances, rich period detail, and themes of perseverance and equality. It has been widely praised for its uplifting tone, strong cast, and historical significance, making it a worthwhile and engaging watch
